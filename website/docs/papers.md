# Paper bank

A traceable reading collection for the survey. Search by title, author, year, or topic, then follow the source and reading prompt.

**Collected does not mean read, understood, or reproduced.** Version labels describe the stored records. This is a starting collection, not an exhaustive bibliography.

<details><summary>How to use this collection</summary><p>For each paper, record its problem, setting, assumptions, objective, algorithm, experimental setup, results, and limitations. Attach section/page references to claims. Use surveys to find original studies, and keep unclear points in your reading notes.</p><p>Eleven reading copies are stored in the repository's ref-papers folder. The links below open official publications or author manuscripts. The original PDF files are not republished by this site.</p></details>
